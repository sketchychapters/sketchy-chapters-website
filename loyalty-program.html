Points & Levels System

Every interaction you have with Workshop Center brings you closer to a higher level and wider perks. This page explains exactly how the system works.

How Do You Earn Points?

Simply put: every course you register for automatically converts into points, added to your balance once your completion is approved by the team.

Complete a Course

Once your course completion is approved, points are added to your balance automatically.

Refer a Friend

Introduce a friend to our center. Once they register and the referral is verified, you earn bonus points.

Share Your Feedback

Share your feedback after completing a course, and receive points as a thank-you for your time.

Review Us on Google & Facebook

Post your review, send us a screenshot of it — and we'll add the points to your account after review.
