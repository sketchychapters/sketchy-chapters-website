About SCDC

Sketchy Chapters Design and Construction was established in 2021 in Lebanon. At SCDC, we provide innovative solutions for value-based project planning and execution.

We provide comprehensive services covering planning, design, engineering, construction, operation and maintenance of buildings and interior design. We have many departments that provide different services (architecture and design, training courses in engineering programs, academic assistance, detailing materials used for construction and design, etc...).
