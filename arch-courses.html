Program Overview:

Use Autodesk Revit for Building Information Modeling (BIM) to enhance efficiency and accuracy throughout the entire project lifecycle; starting from conceptual design, through visualization and analysis, up to execution and construction. Start with high-precision 3D modeling and let the software automatically update plans, elevations, and sections as the model evolves.

Curriculum Syllabus:

  • Introduction to BIM & Autodesk Revit: Interface overview, starting projects, basic view/draw/modify tools, setting up Levels and Grids, adding columns, and linking/importing AutoCAD files.
  • Architectural Design Development: Modeling and modifying walls, doors, windows, and curtain walls, managing plans, elevations, sections, and component elements.
  • Advanced Architectural Elements: Modeling floors, openings, sloped floors, false ceilings, and roofs.
  • Circulation & Documentation: Creating and editing stairs, railings, ramps, working with materials, and basic rendering/output settings.
