Overview:

AutoCAD® is computer-aided design (CAD) software that architects, engineers, and construction professionals rely on to create precise 2D and 3D drawings. Draft, annotate, and design 2D geometry and 3D models with solids, surfaces, and mesh objects. Automate tasks such as drawing comparison, counting, adding blocks, and creating schedules.

Objectives:

  • Navigate throughout AutoCAD using major navigating tools.
  • Understand the concept and techniques to draw.
  • Create multiple designs using several tools & layers.
  • Explain drawing using annotations & plot by scale.
  • Use constraints for certain designs.

Revit Part 1

عدد الجلسات 9 جلسات (3 أسابيع)
التكلفة 149$ حسم 45% (السعر الأصلي: 298$)
تاريخ البدء 17/08

Overview:

Use Revit® BIM (Building Information Modeling) software to drive efficiency and accuracy across the project lifecycle, from conceptual design, visualization, and analysis to fabrication and construction. Begin modeling in 3D with accuracy and precision, automatically update floor plans, elevations, and sections as your model develops, and let Revit handle routine and repetitive tasks with automation.

Objectives:

  • Introduction to BIM and Autodesk Revit: Interface overview, starting projects, viewing commands, basic drawing & modify tools, setting up levels and grids, adding columns, and linking/importing CAD files.
  • Design Development Phase: Modeling & modifying walls, working with doors, windows, and curtain walls, managing views (duplication, callouts, elevations, sections), and adding components.
  • Advanced Architectural Elements: Modeling floors, shaft openings, sloped floors, ceilings, ceiling fixtures, soffits, and roofs by footprint or extrusion.
  • Circulation & Documentation: Creating and modifying component stairs, railings, custom stairs, ramps, working on materials, and rendering types.
